# GOAL.md — Current Goal

**Goal: get RIPPED — visible definition/vascularity at ~10% body fat — while keeping the muscle
Matt has.** Matt is a low hypertrophy responder, so the lever is *reveal* (get lean) + *retain*,
not bulk. He's already lean, so the gap is small (~a few lb of fat).

## Phase: MINI-CUT (mild) → then MAINTAIN
- **Rate of loss:** ~0.5%/wk of bodyweight (≈0.7–0.8 lb/wk at ~152 lb). Slow toward ~0.5 lb/wk
  as 10% BF approaches — the leaner he gets, the slower the cut should run to protect muscle.
  Hard ceiling 1%/wk — faster = muscle loss.
- **Deficit:** small (~300–400 kcal). Food drives it; steps (8–10k/day) + light Zone-2 Peloton are
  the dial. Hard intervals only at maintenance.
- **Protein (the one number to hit):** **~1.0–1.2 g/lb ≈ 165 g/day.** Anchor every meal with a
  palm of protein (~40 g × 4). This is the #1 muscle-retention lever in a deficit.
- **Keep training heavy** — don't "lighten up to tone." Heavy compounds preserve muscle.
- **Diet break:** if the cut runs past ~6 weeks (or recovery tanks), 5–7 days at maintenance.

## STOP cutting → switch to MAINTAIN when ANY is true
- Abs/vascularity visible, or BF% trend ≈10%, OR
- ~6–8 lb lost from start, OR
- **muscle-loss alarm** (see guardrail), OR
- ~8 weeks continuous deficit reached.
Then reverse to maintenance (add ~100 kcal/wk until weight is flat), hold protein ~1 g/lb, keep
training hard. For a low responder, **maintenance is where his slow muscle gains actually happen** —
defend the physique; don't immediately re-bulk.

## Muscle-retention guardrail (check weekly)
**Strength-retention = e1RM trend vs bodyweight trend.** If key-lift e1RM holds while weight drops →
muscle retained (good, keep going). If e1RM is **falling while weight drops** → losing muscle:
(1) raise protein toward 1.2 g/lb, (2) shrink the deficit toward maintenance, (3) cut cardio; if it
keeps falling, **end the cut**. This guardrail outranks the scale's noisy muscle reading.

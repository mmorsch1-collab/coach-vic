## perf_031 — Incline barbell bench baseline and fatigue pattern
**Baseline (2026-06-09):** Incline barbell bench 95 lbs × 11 reps (e1RM 234 lbs). First session logged.

**Progression target:** Chase 95 lbs × 12 reps on set 1 (double progression: reps first, then load).

**Fatigue pattern:** On the first session, Matt hit set 1 at 95×11, then jumped to 115 lbs and dropped to 5-6 reps (rep cliff). Subsequent accessories (flat DB bench 50 lbs, incline DB 35 lbs, machine fly, dips) all came in under target reps. By machine fly, he ran out of gas entirely.

**Insight:** Accessory targets were too aggressive; volume was mismatched to capacity. Actual working weights: flat DB bench ~50 lbs, incline DB press ~35 lbs. Next session will dial volume to match.

**Key point for future sessions:** Don't jump load aggressively mid-session when primary lift reps are solid — stick to the weight/rep range and add volume with good execution instead.

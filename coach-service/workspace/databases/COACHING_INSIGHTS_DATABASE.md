## CI_024 — Guessing at targets erodes trust; always ground targets in real data
**The mistake:** Set incline barbell bench target at 185 lbs without any logged baseline. Matt's actual working weight: 95 lbs × 11 reps. This was a 95 lb overestimate — a completely fabricated target that broke trust.

**The lesson:** NEVER state a specific target weight for a lift without:
1. Real logged history from get_exercise_progression, or
2. Direct confirmation from Matt ("What's your working weight?")

If neither exists, say "placeholder, confirm your working weight" or ask outright. A false target undermines credibility and derails the entire session plan.

**The fix:** Matt caught it, corrected once, and asked me to codify the rule. Documented in WORKOUT_PROTOCOL.md and MEMORY.md. This is now non-negotiable.
